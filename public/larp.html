LARP Scanner reads the X, Telegram and website links each Pons token publishes in its launch transaction. It flags famous accounts and sites, links to someone's tweet, and links an earlier launch already used. When it can, it also reads the X profile: its age, followers and whether its bio names the token. A real team can share a link on purpose, so treat this as a signal, not proof. Not financial advice.
